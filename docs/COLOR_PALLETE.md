# Space Station Exterior Palette

Here’s a sci-fi exterior palette that reads as **advanced, industrial, cold, and believable in space**.

## Core Palette

### 1. Hull White — `#D9E1E8`

Primary body color. Clean, technical, high-visibility against space.

### 2. Titanium Gray — `#8A949E`

Secondary paneling and structural sections. Gives an engineered, aerospace feel.

### 3. Graphite Black — `#2A2F36`

Shadowed recesses, docking rings, vents, and mechanical framing.

### 4. Gunmetal Blue — `#4C6172`

Accent for armor plating and modular segments. Adds cool futuristic depth.

### 5. Signal Cyan — `#6FE7FF`

Lighting strips, navigation indicators, energy ports, and holographic elements.

### 6. Warning Amber — `#FFB347`

Hazard markings, docking guidance, maintenance zones, and caution lines.

### 7. Reactor Red — `#E85D5D`

Emergency lights, alert states, restricted access points, and weapons systems.

### 8. Solar Gold — `#C9A34A`

Optional metallic accent for insignia, elite modules, or command sectors.

## Suggested Usage Ratio

Use this ratio for a strong exterior read:

* **45%** Hull White
* **25%** Titanium Gray
* **15%** Graphite Black
* **10%** Gunmetal Blue
* **5%** accents split between Signal Cyan and Warning Amber
